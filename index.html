Playbook — what separates a real breakout from a rug ›

Signals it's about to pop

  • Existing clout is already adjacent. A known account, KOL wallet, or real public figure with a following sits next to the meme before any coin exists.
  • Outrage or emotional charge attaches to it, especially amplified by a mega-account — moral stakes recruit far more retail attention than a joke alone.
  • Fair-launch, visible dev exit. Full supply public at launch, no presale, dev sells out fast for a token amount — reads as nobody left to dump on you.
  • Coin shows up within hours to days of peak virality, not weeks. Late arrivals are almost always stale copycats.
  • A known wallet buys in publicly, on-chain, within hours — the market trusts a traceable whale more than any tweet.
  • Catalysts stack — the meme, then a trader's rags-to-riches story, then CEX listing rumors. One spike alone tends to fade.

Signals it's a rug or copycat

  • High bundling %. A large share of supply distributed to coordinated/sniper wallets at launch, rather than organic buyers.
  • Concentrated or unknown dev wallet — anon deployer with no track record, still holding a large bag, or linked to a string of other quick launches.
  • Liquidity thin relative to market cap — a market cap built on a shallow pool moves (and can be pulled) on small size.
  • Chart shows spike-and-collapse rather than a base-building structure — most of the move already happened before you could act on it.
  • No path to further catalysts — no listing rumor, no known wallet buying in, no second narrative wave, holder count stagnant or falling.

What each diligence field means

Bundling %
Share of total supply distributed to a cluster of coordinated wallets at launch (snipers/insiders) rather than organic buyers. High bundling = coordinated dump risk.
Dev wallet
Who deployed it and what they've done since — sold out immediately (bullish, "clean"), still holding a large bag (dump risk), or abandoned it entirely (soft rug / CTO candidate).
Narrative strength
How compelling and shareable the underlying story is on its own — independent of whether the token behind it is well-built. A strong narrative on a bad token is still a bad token.
Liquidity / TVL
Real dollars sitting in the trading pool. A market cap resting on thin liquidity is fragile — it can be pumped or drained with comparatively little capital.
Market conditions
The macro backdrop (BTC trend, overall risk appetite, Solana meme-sector momentum) that decides whether any given launch can catch a sustained bid at all, regardless of how good the meme is.
Holder count
Breadth of ownership. A handful of wallets holding most of supply is a concentration/dump risk even if the headline market cap looks large.
Chart structure
Where in its lifecycle the price action sits — early discovery, parabolic spike, base-building, or already collapsed. Determines whether there's realistically anything left to catch.

Method: every 8 hours this fans out parallel research agents — one scanning viral animal content, one scanning viral AI content, one scanning crypto-KOL/launch-tracker chatter from the last 24–72h — then a diligence pass tries to pull bundling %, dev-wallet history, liquidity/TVL, holder count, and chart structure for anything that already has a coin, plus a market-conditions read. It searches the open web (news aggregators, crypto trackers, wiki/fandom pages); it does not have a live X/Twitter firehose or authenticated access to on-chain tools like RugCheck/DexScreener/Solscan (those block direct fetches from here), so figures marked "unverified" are genuine gaps, not rounding — verify anything you'd act on directly against the contract before trading.

Not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely high-risk, illiquid, and often engineered to transfer money from latecomers to early wallets and anonymous deployers — the large majority go to zero or get rugged, as several entries on this exact page already have.
